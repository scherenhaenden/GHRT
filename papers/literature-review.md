# Primary Literature and Comparison with GHRT

This list establishes background and constraints; **no citation automatically validates GHRT**.

1. Hinterbichler, Khoury, Levy, and Matas (2011), “Symmetron Cosmology”, *Phys. Rev. D* **84**, 103521. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.84.103521 — environmental potential and screening background.
2. Coleman and De Luccia (1980), “Gravitational Effects on and of Vacuum Decay”, *Phys. Rev. D* **21**, 3305. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.21.3305 — vacuum tunneling with gravity.
3. Farhi and Guth (1987), “An obstacle to creating a universe in the laboratory”, *Phys. Lett. B* **183**, 149. https://doi.org/10.1016/0370-2693(87)90429-1 — classical obstacles to locally created inflationary universes.
4. Farhi, Guth, and Guven (1990), “Is it possible to create a universe in the laboratory by quantum tunneling?”, *Nucl. Phys. B* **339**, 417–490. https://doi.org/10.1016/0550-3213(90)90357-J — tunneling hypothesis toward an inflationary universe.
5. Borde, Guth, and Vilenkin (2003), “Inflationary Spacetimes Are Incomplete in Past Directions”, *Phys. Rev. Lett.* **90**, 151301. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.90.151301 — past completeness bound under theorem conditions.
6. Planck Collaboration (2020), “Planck 2018 results. VI. Cosmological parameters”, *A&A* **641**, A6. https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201833910 — $\Lambda$CDM reference; not a GHRT prediction.
7. Barbieri, Brinckmann, Gariazzo, Lattanzi, Pastor, and Pisanti (2025), “Current Constraints on Cosmological Scenarios with Very Low Reheating Temperatures”, *Phys. Rev. Lett.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.01369 and https://doi.org/10.1103/j5rj-dz1k — scenario-dependent bound $T_{\rm RH} > 5.96\text{ MeV}$ (95%).
8. Adams and Laughlin (1997), “A Dying Universe: The Long-Term Fate and Evolution of Astrophysical Objects”, *Rev. Mod. Phys.* **69**, 337. https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.69.337 — cosmological eras beyond stellar lifetimes.
9. Krauss and Starkman (2000), “Life, The Universe, and Nothing: Life and Death in an Ever-Expanding Universe”, *Astrophys. J.* **531**, 22. https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9902189 — finite resources and accelerated cosmology.
10. Dyson (1979), “Time Without End: Physics and Biology in an Open Universe”, *Rev. Mod. Phys.* **51**, 447. https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.51.447 — deferred computation under specific cosmological assumptions.
11. Hoang, Lazarian, Burkhart, and Loeb (2017), “The Interaction of Relativistic Spacecrafts with the Interstellar Medium”, *Astrophys. J.* **837**, 5. https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.05284 — dust and gas effects at relativistic speeds.
12. Sakharov (1967), “Violation of CP Invariance, C Asymmetry, and Baryon Asymmetry of the Universe”, *JETP Lett.* **5**, 24 — historical baryogenesis criteria.

Do not use popular-science videos as primary evidence for a technical result. Claims about radii, rates, nucleation probabilities, information migration, or AI technology are custom calculations or conjectures, not results contained in these references.
