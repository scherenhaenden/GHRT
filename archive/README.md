# GHRT Provenance Archive — 2026-11-21

These texts are preserved **without scientific correction of their original Spanish wording**, to allow comparing the project's history against refined documents. Do not read them as validated results.

- `Reformular_teoria_GHRT_50_problemas_original.txt`: historical analysis of 50 problems and their conceptual grouping; percentages indicated informal constructibility assessments, **not** probabilities of physical truth.
- `GHRT_2_0_adenda_original.md`: first formulation of GHRT-VPT with a sixth-order potential, tuned-rate benchmark, and open problems; contains explicit approximations and does not incorporate all subsequent auditing.
- `Gemini_analisis_recibido_sin_verificar.txt`: evaluation text attributed to Gemini and supplied in conversation. Contains unverified claims and exaggerations, including assumptions about AI survival, portals, and baryogenesis; it is a historical record of the discussion and **not a primary scientific source**.

For current corrections, consult `docs/12-vpt-model-and-calculations.md` and `docs/14-audit-and-corrections-2026-11-21.md`. Accurate attribution is preserved: the critical intuition about an AI that could do more before the universe ages came from the author, who later discussed it with Gemini.
