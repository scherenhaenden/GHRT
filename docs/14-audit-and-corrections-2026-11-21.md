# 14 — Critical Audit of GHRT and the Gemini Dialogue (2026-11-21)

**Finding types:** C = calculation or definition correction; E = unwarranted extrapolation; O = open question. This note does not replace peer review.

## C1. One field, two incompatible mass scales in the benchmark

Potential $W = a y^2/2 - y^4/4 + y^6/6$. In the false vacuum, $m_\chi^2 = a E_*^4 / f^2$. With $a = 0.180$, $E_* = 2.240365\text{ meV}$, and $f \approx 0.410822\text{ meV}$:
$m_\chi \approx 5.183 \times 10^{-3}\text{ eV}$.
$\hbar H_\infty \approx 1.190 \times 10^{-33}\text{ eV}$.
$m_\chi / (\hbar H_\infty) \approx 4.36 \times 10^{30}$.

A field trapped in a false vacuum can act like $\Lambda$ even when heavy, but it is invalid to use this same benchmark as a demonstration of a scalar slow-rolling on Hubble scales. Study two fields—cosmological $\chi$ and transition $\varphi$—without assuming that doing so fixes naturalness.

## C2. Environmental coupling tension

If $\Delta a = \rho_* f^2 / (M^2 E_*^4)$, with $\rho_m/E_*^4 \approx \Omega_m/\Omega_\Lambda \approx 0.459854$ and target $\Delta a = 0.01$, then $M \approx 2.79\text{ meV}$. The conformal factor $A = \exp(\chi^2 / (2M^2))$ gives at $\chi = f y_+$ a coupling $\beta \approx 1.13 \times 10^{29}$; this must be confronted with screening/fifth-force bounds. For $M = M_{\rm Pl}$, $\Delta a \approx 1.31 \times 10^{-62}$. Do not present “density engineering” as a solved viable technique.

## C3. Action $B$ in the example was tuned to a target

$B \approx 252724\,(f/E_*)^4$ corresponds to the flat thin-wall approximation with $\sigma$ estimated at $a_{\rm degenerate} = 3/16$ and evaluated at $a = 0.180$. We **chose** $\varepsilon = 0.001$ and $\mathcal{A} \approx E_*^4$ to deduce $B_{\rm obj} \approx 285.75$ and $f \approx 0.411\text{ meV}$. It is not an independently predicted rate. If $f \sim M_{\rm Pl}$ and $E_* \sim \text{meV}$, $B_{\rm flat} \sim 10^{125}$ falls outside the regime where the flat formula can be used without gravity.

## E1. Three non-equivalent phenomena

A decay bubble from false to true vacuum is not automatically a high-energy false-vacuum bubble built inside a low-energy universe. Nor is it automatically a hot Big Bang or a daughter spacetime. Distinguish CDL and Farhi–Guth–Guven constructions.

## E2. Manipulating $\rho_*$ does not automatically trigger tunneling

With the adopted sign, $\partial a / \partial\rho_* > 0$; emptying reduces $a$, concentrating increases it. $a = 0$ is the local classical stability limit. A finite cavity requires gradients, size, and boundary conditions. “Lower barrier” is not a known rate without a bounce calculation.

## C4. Insufficient thermal budget

Present dark energy equivalent to $E_* = 2.240\text{ meV}$ gives a maximum photon temperature $\approx 28.87\text{ K}$; the difference $\Delta U = 0.002840\,E_*^4$ alone gives $\approx 6.66\text{ K}$. With $g_* = 10.75$ and $T_{\rm RH} = 5.96\text{ MeV}$, the ratio $u_{\rm rad}/u_\Lambda \approx 1.77 \times 10^{38}$. There is no hot Big Bang without another energy sector. The $5.96\text{ MeV}$ bound comes from a specific cosmological analysis, not an exact universal constant.

## E3. CP-even portal does not generate CP violation

$\mathcal{L}_{\rm portal} = -\lambda (H^\dagger H) F(\chi)$, with real, CP-even $F$ and parameters, does not violate CP automatically. Having an out-of-equilibrium wall does not establish the other Sakharov conditions. It requires a nontrivial CP sector and an asymmetry calculation; $\eta_B \approx 6 \times 10^{-10}$ is an observational target.

## E4. Phase variations do not necessarily imply antimatter domains

Matter/antimatter domains, topological walls, and local variations of CP sources are different phenomena. Do not assume that the CP sign forms a persistent mosaic without studying the potential, correlations, and thermal dynamics.

## C5. Global growth is not individual continuity

In the ideal de Sitter model:
$V_F \propto \exp[(3 - 4\pi\varepsilon/3)Ht]$ with $\varepsilon < 9/(4\pi)$ implies growing expected volume, not a universal percolation threshold;
$P_{\rm line} \approx \exp[-4\pi\varepsilon Ht/3]$ for a given worldline.
$\varepsilon = 0.001$ gives $\tau \approx 4.18 \times 10^{12}\text{ yr}$ and $P$ after $10^{14}\text{ yr} \approx 4.18 \times 10^{-11}$ (probability, not %). A “plumber” needs to solve survival, replication, and communication in addition to global volume.

## E5. Information transfer not demonstrated

Daughter-universe geometry can contain causal barriers; “encoding something in the wall” is not enough. Interior observables and an initial-data map must be identified. Survival of consciousness/identity adds a philosophical question not resolved by a field action.

## E6. Videos, AI, and late-time energy

The original question of AI survival was raised by the author after watching the video, not originally suggested by Gemini. The end of stars at $\sim 10^{13}\text{–}10^{14}\text{ yr}$ does not immediately exhaust black holes and other energy sources; this does not guarantee infinite computation. $T_{\rm GH} \approx 2.20 \times 10^{-30}\text{ K}$ and $E_{\rm Landauer} \approx 2.10 \times 10^{-53}\text{ J}$ per irreversible bit erasure at that $T$ under an ideal model. Controlled FTL lacks a proven mechanism; neither has it been proven impossible for every imaginable geometry with exotic conditions, so both unwarranted certainties should be avoided.

## C6. Relativistic impact

A $1\text{ g}$ projectile at $0.1c$ carries $\sim 4.53 \times 10^{11}\text{ J} \approx 108.2\text{ t TNT}$; classical approximation $\sim 107.4\text{ t}$. **Energy is correct**, but precise damage, “clean” penetration, radiation dose, and impact probability do not follow from energy alone.

## O1–O6. Unresolved problems

- **O1:** covariance of a degravitation filter and its compatibility with inflation;
- **O2:** quantum stability and naturalness of the scalar;
- **O3:** screening in the radiation era and thermal shifts;
- **O4:** boundary conditions/action of the CDL bounce and interior cosmology;
- **O5:** dynamical origin of perturbations, thermal history, and baryogenesis;
- **O6:** causality/entropy/information in a daughter universe.

**Conclusion:** GHRT-VPT can formulate calculable questions, but there is currently no complete solution, predicted rates, distinctive observation, or demonstration of universe engineering. Historical constructibility percentages are not probabilities of truth.
