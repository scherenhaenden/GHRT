# 00 — Concept and Provenance of Ideas

**Status: exploratory research program; not a confirmed physical theory.**

Granular Higgs Recoil Theory (GHRT) asks whether a scalar-field formulation can exist in which the material environment, the trace of the stress-energy tensor, and a possible coupling to the Higgs sector modify gravitational or cosmological responses. The words “granular”, “recoil”, and the image of an expansion tension describe the **original intuition**—rooted in the author's early childhood reasoning (ages 4–6) that physical objects cannot possess strictly zero rest motion/energy (like a parked car vibrating at idle, or the undefined ratio of finite speed to zero) and that smaller structures require disproportionately higher energy and finer tools to break or probe—not derived microphysical properties. No proven discrete spacetime or first-principles “recoil” force has been established here.

## Five candidate cores

(I) Scalar coupling to the trace $T = T^\mu{}_\mu$; (II) environmental effective potential and screening; (III) possible degravitation of strictly constant vacuum components; (IV) an explicit Higgs portal; (V) quantum protection of the ultralight scalar. Mechanisms I–II have analogies with published symmetron-type theories and must not be presented as exclusive discoveries of GHRT.

## GHRT-VPT extension, 2026-11-21

Building on the author's question about a persistent expanding background, gravity, quantum fluctuations, and local Big Bangs, a scenario of **first-order vacuum transitions** and daughter regions was added. Its closest analogy is false-vacuum eternal inflation, not the historical Steady State model. The extension still requires dynamical gravity, an inflaton/reheating sector, perturbations, and confrontation with observations.

## Origin of the intelligence discussion

The author watched the video *“The Next 100 Trillion Years After AI Wiped Out Humanity”* and **questioned on their own initiative** why an extraordinarily advanced AI would accept extinction when the observable universe ages. The author proposed investigating whether knowledge accumulated over cosmic timescales could allow manipulating conditions of vacuum fluctuations/transitions, renewing cosmological regions, and preserving existence or information. A subsequent conversation with Gemini explored these ideas. Do not attribute the author's original intuition to Gemini, and do not infer that conceptual possibility equals technological capability.

We distinguish: (a) creating a bubble; (b) achieving inflation and a hot Big Bang; (c) creating an independent daughter geometry; (d) transferring usable information; (e) preserving subjective continuity of an AI. These are different problems. **There is no demonstrated mechanism solving (b)–(e).**
