# 05 — Screening and Fifth Force

Reference symmetron potential:

$$
U_{\rm eff}(\chi;\rho_{\ast}) = U_0 + \left(\frac{\rho_{\ast}}{M^2} - \mu^2\right)\frac{\chi^2}{2} + \frac{\lambda}{4}\chi^4.
$$

Here $\rho_c = \mu^2 M^2$. If $\rho_{\ast} > \rho_c$, $\chi = 0$ is the minimum; for $\rho_{\ast} < \rho_c$, the minima satisfy $\chi^2 = (\mu^2 - \rho_{\ast}/M^2)/\lambda$. This is a **continuous** pattern of symmetry breaking/restoration, not an automatic example of a first-order quantum transition. Primary literature: Hinterbichler et al., *Phys. Rev. D* **84**, 103521 (2011), https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.84.103521.

For $A = \exp(\chi^2 / (2M^2))$, $\alpha = \chi / M^2$ and $\beta = M_{\rm Pl}\chi / M^2$. The linear scalar force for point sources with equal effective coupling, in a constant-mass background, satisfies:

$$
\frac{F_\chi}{F_N} = 2\beta_{\rm eff}^2 (1 + m_{\rm eff} r) e^{-m_{\rm eff} r}.
$$

This formula depends on the linear regime; extended and screened bodies require field profiles and effective charges. Do not infer viability solely from a local minimum or a large mass.

## GHRT-VPT benchmark tension

For the benchmark $a = 0.180$, $E_{\ast} = 2.240\text{ meV}$, $f \approx 0.411\text{ meV}$, the environmental coupling $\Delta a = \rho_{\ast} f^2 / (M^2 E_{\ast}^4)$ requires $M \approx 2.79\text{ meV}$ to produce $\Delta a = 0.01$ at mean cosmological matter density. At the nonzero minimum this gives $|\beta| \sim 1.13 \times 10^{29}$, signaling severe tension with fifth-force bounds unless screening is extraordinarily effective. With $M = M_{\rm Pl}$ and the same $f$, $\Delta a \approx 1.31 \times 10^{-62}$: environmental control is negligible at cosmological matter density. This demonstrates parameter tension; it does **not** automatically exclude every function $A$ or other mechanisms.
